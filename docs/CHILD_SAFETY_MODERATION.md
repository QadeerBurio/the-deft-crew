# Restricted child safety moderation procedure

This procedure is for designated tdc child safety responders. Before rollout, the owner must confirm who monitors privacy@gettdc.pk, who provides backup coverage, and who can authorize provider and authority actions. Legal counsel must confirm jurisdiction, reporting deadlines, authorized evidence handling, and retention rules.

1. Review urgent and high-priority cases in **Safety → Safety concerns**. Assign a responder, mark the case under review, and assess immediate risk. Escalate imminent danger using the approved emergency procedure.
2. Limit case access to designated accounts with `safety.intake.view` and `safety.intake.act`. Account actions also require `students.suspend`. Use case references and factual notes. Avoid copying sensitive report details or suspected material into email, tickets, logs, or chat.
3. Restrict access to suspected prohibited content using the approved incident procedure. The portal can hide confessions and remove supported content from the app. Direct storage URLs and CDN caches require a separate provider check. If an object is still reachable, an authorized storage operator must restrict provider access and invalidate relevant caches according to the approved evidence procedure. Confirm public access has stopped using a harmless fixture during staging.
4. For confirmed material, follow the approved evidence and removal procedure. Preserve only the records counsel and the receiving authority authorize. Record content identifiers, timestamps, access restrictions, and decision history in the restricted case. Avoid downloading, screenshotting, forwarding, or re-uploading suspected material.
5. Suspend or ban an account only after authorized review. Validate the account-status enforcement flag for the affected population and verify enforcement across sign-in, authenticated HTTP and relevant chat/socket paths. Test existing-session behavior during staging.
6. The designated responder handles authority reporting through the approved channel. Use the applicable authority and timeframe established by legal review. Mark the case **Reported externally** only after the authorized responder completes the report. Record the date and a non-sensitive receipt reference.
7. Resolve the case after documenting decisions, storage access checks, account enforcement, and any external submission. Keep case records and evidence references under the approved access and retention rules.
